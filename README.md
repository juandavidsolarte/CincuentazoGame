A strategic card game where one human player competes against 1–3 opponents.
The goal is to survive without letting the table sum exceed 50, playing cards intelligently until you are the last player standing.
>  Universidad del Valle.

---

##  Game Rules

Each player starts with 4 cards.

There is an accumulated sum on the table that must not exceed 50.

When playing a card:

2–8, 10 : add their value

9 → adds nothing (neutral)

J, Q, K : subtract 10

A : adds 1 or 10 (whichever is most convenient)

If a player cannot play any card without exceeding 50, they are eliminated.

The winner is the last active player.
